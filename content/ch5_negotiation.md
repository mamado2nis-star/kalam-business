### A short introduction
Negotiating in English can be challenging, especially for non-native speakers.
However, with the right approach and techniques, it can also be an opportunity to showcase your language skills and build stronger relationships with your colleagues and clients.
In this context, here are some useful tips and strategies to help you navigate negotiations in English with confidence and success.
1. Brush up on your vocabulary: Make sure you have a good understanding of the specific vocabulary used in negotiations. This will help you to communicate your position more effectively.
2. Know your goals: Before entering a negotiation, be clear about what you want to achieve. This will help you to focus on your priorities and prevent you from getting sidetracked.
3. Be confident: Confidence is key in negotiations. Speak clearly, maintain eye contact and use assertive language to convey your position.
4. Listen carefully: Active listening is crucial in negotiations. Pay close attention to what the other party is saying and ask questions to clarify any points of confusion.
5. Be flexible: Negotiations often require compromise. Be prepared to adjust your position and seek creative solutions to achieve a mutually beneficial outcome.
6. Keep emotions in check: Negotiations can be tense, but it's important to keep your emotions in check. Stay calm and composed, even if the other party becomes heated or aggressive.
7. Practice, practice, practice: Like any skill, negotiation takes practice to master. Look for opportunities to negotiate in English, whether in your personal or professional life, and continue to refine your approach over time.
### Story 1
Let's start with a real-life scenario about negotiation.
In the following scenario, the CEO of a manufacturing company, Mr. Hashem El-Deeb, is negotiating a contract with the sales officer, Mr. Fred Jackson, to produce a new line of smartphones that will be sold under their brand name.
The supplier is a large multinational corporation known for producing high-quality components.
In this scenario, the negotiation process involved making offers, emphasizing deal breakers, agreeing and disagreeing, stalling and overcoming blockages.
Through open communication and compromise, both parties were able to reach a mutually beneficial agreement and close the business negotiation successfully.
Hashem: Thank you for meeting with me today to discuss our new smartphone line. We're interested in purchasing 500,000 units at a price of $200 per unit. We would also like the supplier to be responsible for all shipping costs.
Fred: Thank you for considering our company as your supplier. However, I'm afraid that the cost of producing the new components is quite high, and we would need to charge $250 per unit.
Hashem: I appreciate your concern, but I'm afraid that this price is too high for us. We are looking for a more competitive offer. Can you offer a better price?
Fred: We understand your position, but we cannot lower our price any further. Our production costs are quite high, and we need to ensure a profit margin.
Hashem: I see. Well, we have other options available to us, so we may need to consider finding a different supplier if we cannot reach a mutually beneficial agreement.
Fred: I understand. However, I want to emphasize that our components are of the highest quality, and we have a reputation for producing reliable products. We also offer a 5% discount for orders over one million units.
Hashem: I appreciate your offer, but we still need to consider the overall cost. Can you offer any other incentives or adjustments to help us reach a more favorable agreement?
Fred: We could offer a volume discount of 15% if you purchase more than two million units. We could also provide a 90-day payment term to help with cash flow.
Hashem: That's an interesting offer. However, we need to ensure that the final price is in line with our budget. Can we revisit the price and shipping costs and see if we can find a compromise?
Fred: Of course. I understand that this is an important decision for your company. Let's review the numbers and see if we can find a solution that works for both parties.
Hashem: Thank you for your understanding. I believe that we can reach a mutually beneficial agreement if we work together to find a solution that meets both of our needs.
Fred: Agreed. Let's keep discussing and see if we can come up with a solution that works for both of us.
Hashem: I appreciate your efforts to find a solution, but I'm afraid that the delivery timeline is a critical aspect of this agreement. If we cannot meet our requirements for delivery, we won't be able to proceed with the contract.
Fred: I understand your position, but the timeline is quite tight. We would need to add additional resources and expenses to meet your requirements.
Hashem: I see. Well, this is a critical requirement for us, so we won't be able to proceed with the agreement if we cannot meet the deadline. Can you think of any other options that might help us meet this requirement?
Fred: We could explore hiring additional staff to work on your project or outsourcing some of the work to a third-party vendor. However, this would increase the overall cost.
Hashem: I appreciate your suggestions, but we need to ensure that the timeline is met while still keeping the overall cost within our budget. Let's continue to explore options and see if we can find a solution that works for both of us.
Fred: After considering all of the factors, we are willing to offer you a price of $220 per unit and a 10% discount for orders over one million units. We can also guarantee delivery within your required timeline by allocating additional resources to the project.
Hashem: That sounds like a reasonable offer. Thank you for working with us to find a solution that meets our needs. We accept your offer.
Fred: Great. I'm pleased that we were able to reach an agreement. I believe this partnership will be beneficial for both of our organizations.
Hashem: I agree. Thank you for your time and effort in negotiating with us. We look forward to a successful partnership and the launch of our new smartphone line.
Fred: Likewise. Thank you for choosing us as your supplier. We will work hard to meet and exceed your expectations.
Hashem: Thank you. Have a great day.
Fred: You too. Bye.
The negotiation scenario presented a realistic and comprehensive example of a business negotiation.
The dialogue included a range of situations, such as making offers, emphasizing deal breakers and overcoming blockages, which are commonly encountered in real-life negotiations.
The CEO and supplier demonstrated effective communication skills by actively listening to each other's concerns and proposing potential solutions to reach a mutually beneficial agreement.
The negotiation process involved open dialogue, compromise and a focus on finding common ground.
Additionally, the dialogue highlighted the importance of maintaining a positive working relationship with the supplier, even when negotiations become challenging.
Both parties expressed satisfaction with the final agreement and look forward to a successful partnership.
Overall, the dialogue serves as a useful example of effective negotiation strategies and communication skills in a business setting.

### Some expressions you can use to buy time, stall or be non-committal during business negotiations

I'm going to need some time to think this over.

I would like to explore some other options before making a commitment.
Let me get back to you by the end of the week.

Can we schedule another meeting to discuss this further?

It can be important to be non-committal or vague during negotiations, especially if you need more time to think about an offer or if you are not yet ready to make a firm commitment.
Additionally, being non-committal or vague can give you more leverage in the negotiation, as it keeps the other party guessing and can help you avoid revealing too much information too early.
However, it's important to strike a balance and not be too non-committal or vague, as this can lead to misunderstandings or mistrust between the negotiating parties.
