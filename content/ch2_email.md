# Chapter 2 — Business Email

## 1. The structure of a typical business email (p. 121–124)
Audio: em_121

When it comes to the structure of a business email, there are a few things to keep in mind.
First, make sure your subject line is clear and catchy. You want to grab the reader's attention.
Next up is the greeting. This is where you say hello and set the tone for the rest of the email. Depending on who you're emailing, you might use "Hi", "Hello" or "Dear".
Then it's time for the body of the email. This is where you get to share your message. Keep it clear and organized so the reader can easily understand what you're saying. You can use bullet points, paragraphs, or a mix of both to get your point across.
When it comes time to wrap things up, don't forget to include a closing line. This is where you politely sign off and thank the reader for their time. "Regards", "Thanks for your help" or "Take care" are all good options.
Be very careful choosing Reply or Reply All.
Check any attachments before you hit Send.
Finally, make sure to include your signature at the end. This is where you share your name, job title and contact info. And if you have a cool logo or photo to include, go for it.
By following this simple structure, you can write business emails that are clear, effective and friendly.
### Example email
Subject: Quick question about the project deadline
Hi Jane, hope you're having a great day!
I'm reaching out to you today to ask a quick question about the project deadline. As you know, the deadline is coming up soon, and I want to make sure we're on track to hit it.
I have a few questions about the timeline and deliverables, and I was hoping you could help me out. Specifically, I'm wondering: Are we still on track to deliver the final report by the end of next week? Do we have all the information we need from the client to move forward with the project? Is there anything I can do to help ensure we hit the deadline?
I appreciate your help with this, and I look forward to hearing back from you soon.
Best regards,

## 2. Greetings and introductions (p. 125–133)
Audio: em_125

### Formal greetings
If you know the name of the recipient: Dear Mr. ___ · Dear Ms. ___ · Dear Mrs. ___ · Dear Dr. ___ · Dear Professor ___
If you don't know the name of the recipient: Dear Hiring Manager · Dear Sir · Dear Madam
### Very formal greetings
Esteemed · Respected · Honorable
Real-life examples: Esteemed Professor Johnson · Respected CEO · Honorable Ambassador Smith · Esteemed Director Brown
### Semi-formal greetings
Hello · Hi · Good morning · Good afternoon · Good evening · Dear ___ · Hi there
### Introductions
The following are some common types of introductions used in business emails.
Introduction of yourself: My name is ___, and I work for ___. I wanted to introduce myself and express my interest in working with your company. I'm reaching out to you because I think our companies could benefit from working together.
Introduction of the topic: I'm writing to you today to discuss ___. The purpose of this email is to address ___. I wanted to bring to your attention the issue of ___.
Introduction of a problem: I wanted to bring to your attention the problem we've been experiencing with ___. I'm reaching out to you because we've encountered a problem with ___. Unfortunately, we've run into some issues with ___.
Introduction of a solution: I've been working on a solution to the issue we've been having with ___. I'm writing to share my proposal for ___. After careful consideration, I believe I've found a solution to ___.
Introduction of a request: I'm writing to request ___. I wanted to ask if it would be possible to ___. Could you please ___?
Introduction of a referral: I was referred to you by ___, who spoke highly of your work. ___ recommended that I contact you regarding ___. I wanted to reach out to you based on the recommendation of ___.
Introduction of a follow-up: I'm writing to follow up on our conversation last ___. As a follow-up to our previous email exchange, I wanted to ___. I wanted to check in and see if there have been any updates since our last conversation.
Introduction of a thank-you: I wanted to start by thanking you for ___. Thank you for your help with ___. I'm writing to express my gratitude for ___.
Introduction of a reminder: Just a friendly reminder that ___. I wanted to remind you of ___. As a quick reminder, ___.
Introduction of congratulations: I wanted to start by congratulating you on ___. Congratulations on your recent ___. I'm writing to express my congratulations on ___.
Introduction of a recommendation: I wanted to recommend ___ for ___. If you're looking for someone with expertise in ___, I highly recommend ___. I wanted to recommend my colleague ___ for ___.
Introduction of a question: I'm writing to ask if ___. Could you please provide more information on ___? I was wondering if you could help me with ___.
Introduction of an apology: I wanted to start by apologizing for ___. I'm writing to apologize for ___. Please accept my apologies for ___.
Introduction of a suggestion: I'm writing to suggest ___. Have you considered ___? I wanted to share my idea for ___.
Introduction of a clarification: I wanted to clarify ___. I'm writing to provide some additional information and clarification on ___. Just to clarify, ___.
Introduction of a collaboration: I'm writing to discuss the possibility of collaborating on ___. I wanted to explore the idea of collaborating with ___. Have you considered a collaborative approach for ___?
Introduction of an agreement: I wanted to confirm our agreement on ___. Just to confirm our understanding, we have agreed to ___. I'm writing to acknowledge our agreement on ___.
### Important note: friendly opening lines
I hope this email finds you well.
I trust this email reaches you in good spirits.
I hope everything is going well for you.
Wishing you a pleasant day ahead.
I hope all is well with you.

## 3. The email body, part one (p. 134–138)

### Referencing attachments
Please find attached ___ for your review and consideration.
I have attached ___ to this email, which includes the information and details we discussed.
Here is the information you requested, attached as ___, which I hope will be useful in your decision-making process.
Please see the attached ___, which contains the necessary information and data on ___.
### Providing background or context for a discussion
As a brief recap, we discussed ___.
In regards to the issue we talked about earlier, ___.
As you may recall, we previously discussed ___.
To give you some background, the project was initiated to address ___.
I wanted to provide some additional information on this topic, which may help you understand ___.
### Sharing updates or news about a project
I wanted to provide you with an update on the status of our project.
We have made significant progress on the project since our last update.
I am pleased to share that we have achieved the latest milestone.
As promised, here is an update on the project's timeline and progress.
I wanted to let you know that we're on track to meet the project's objectives.
I'm writing to share some exciting new developments in our project.
### Communicating details about an event or meeting
Just a friendly reminder that our event/meeting is taking place on ___.
Here are the details for the event/meeting.
We have made some changes to the agenda for the event/meeting; please see the attached updated version.
Here are the details for the virtual event/meeting, including the login information.
### Providing instructions or directions
To get started, please refer to the attached document, which provides detailed instructions.
To ensure that you complete the task/project correctly, please pay close attention to the following steps.
To access the file/information you need, please follow these steps.
Please make sure you follow the instructions/directions carefully to avoid any errors or delays.
To navigate the new system/software, please follow these instructions.
### Asking for assistance or support with a project or task
Could you please help me with ___ on the project?
I am struggling with ___ and could use your support in finding a solution.
Would you be able to provide some assistance with ___?
I was hoping to get your input on ___ related to the project/task.
### Requesting a meeting or phone call to discuss a topic
I am writing to request a meeting/call to discuss ___.
I would appreciate the opportunity to schedule a meeting/call with you to discuss ___.
Could we schedule a call to discuss ___ at your earliest convenience?
Would you be available to schedule a meeting/call to discuss ___ in the next few days/weeks?
### Seeking approval or authorization for a decision
I am writing to request your approval for ___.
Could you please authorize ___ so that we can move forward with the project/task?
I am seeking your approval for ___, as it is critical for the success of the project/task.
In order to proceed with ___, I need your authorization to ensure that we're in compliance with ___.
### Clarifying a misunderstanding
I wanted to clarify a point from our previous conversation/email.
There seems to have been a misunderstanding about ___, and I wanted to clarify.
I think there may have been some confusion about ___, so I wanted to provide some clarification.
I apologize if there was any confusion or misunderstanding about ___, and I wanted to clarify.

## 4. The email body, part two (p. 139–145)

### Expressing gratitude

### Apologizing
I realize that my actions may have caused inconvenience or hardship, and I want to express my apologies.
I am sorry for any frustration or confusion that my actions may have caused.

### Discussing pricing or payment terms
I wanted to discuss the pricing/payment terms for ___ services/products.
Could we schedule a call to go over the pricing/payment terms in more detail?
Would it be possible to adjust the pricing/payment terms to better align with our budget/needs?
### Negotiating a contract or agreement
I would like to discuss some potential changes or adjustments to the contract/agreement.
I would like to propose some alternative terms for the contract/agreement that may better align with our needs/interests.
Would it be possible to add/amend a specific clause or provision in the contract/agreement to better protect our interests?
### Addressing a problem or challenge that needs to be resolved
I wanted to draw your attention to a problem/challenge that needs to be addressed as soon as possible.
We're facing a challenge/problem with ___, and I would like to work together to find a solution.
I am committed to finding a resolution for ___, and I would appreciate your input and support.
I understand that this problem/challenge requires immediate attention, and I am working to address it as quickly and effectively as possible.
### Expressing interest in a job opportunity or position
I am writing to express my interest in the ___ position at ___ and to share my qualifications and experience.
I was excited to see the ___ opening at ___, and I would like to be considered for the role.
As someone with a strong background in ___, I am confident that I would be a great fit for ___.
I am impressed by the mission and values of ___, and I believe my skills and experience align well with the requirements of ___.
### Requesting more information about a product or service
I would like to request more information about ___, including its features, pricing and availability.
Could you please provide more details about ___ and how it can benefit my business/organization?
I am interested in learning more about ___, and I would appreciate any additional information or resources you can provide.
### Providing feedback
I wanted to offer some feedback on ___ and to share my thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
I appreciate the hard work and dedication that has gone into ___, and I would like to offer some constructive feedback to help us achieve our goals.
I would like to offer some feedback on ___ and to discuss potential solutions or next steps to address any concerns.
### Asking for feedback
I would appreciate your feedback on ___ and would love to hear your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
Could you please provide some feedback on ___ and share any observations or ideas for improvement?
### Making an announcement
I am delighted to inform you that ___.
I wanted to bring to your attention that ___.
I wanted to update you on some recent developments.
I am writing to let you know that ___.
I have some news to share regarding ___.
### Responding to a complaint
I apologize for any inconvenience or frustration caused by ___, and I appreciate you taking the time to bring it to our attention.
Thank you for bringing this matter to our attention, and please know that we take your concerns very seriously.
We're committed to addressing and resolving any concerns or issues raised by our customers, and I would like to work with you to find a satisfactory solution.
Please let me know if there are any specific actions we can take to address your concerns or make things right.
### Complaining
I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with ___ and to request your assistance in resolving this matter.
I am disappointed with the level of service provided by ___, and I would like to discuss a possible resolution.
I would like to bring to your attention a problem I have encountered with ___ and to seek your help in finding a solution.
### Congratulating someone
Congratulations on ___! It is well deserved and a testament to your hard work and dedication.
I wanted to extend my warmest congratulations on ___ and to express my admiration for your accomplishments.
I am thrilled to hear about your success in ___, and I wanted to reach out to offer my sincere congratulations and best wishes.
### Urging action
I wanted to remind you of the urgency of ___ and to request your prompt attention to this matter.
Please take immediate action on ___, as it is critical to our success and progress.
It is vital that we take action on ___ in a timely and effective manner, and I would appreciate your support in this effort.

## 5. Closings and common abbreviations (p. 146–149)
Audio: em_146

### Closings
Formal closings when emailing someone for the first time: Sincerely · Respectfully · Cordially
Formal closings when you have an ongoing line of communication: Best regards · Kind regards · Warm regards · Regards · With appreciation
Informal closings: Take care · Cheers · Best · Thanks · Talk to you soon · Have a great day · See you later · Until next time
Thank-you closings: Thank you for your time. · Thank you for your attention to this matter. · Thank you for your time and consideration.
Call-to-action closings: I look forward to hearing from you. · Please let me know your thoughts. · I'd love to discuss this further with you. · Please don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions. · Let's schedule a meeting to discuss this in more detail. · Could you please confirm that you've received this message?
### The most common abbreviations you might see in business emails
ASAP: as soon as possible · FYI: for your information · EOD: end of day · EOW: end of week
TBD: to be determined · ETA: estimated time of arrival · ETD: estimated time of departure
IT: information technology · KPI: key performance indicator · OOO: out of office · PTO: paid time off
ROI: return on investment · SOP: standard operating procedure · TBA: to be announced · TBC: to be confirmed
VIP: very important person · AKA: also known as · CEO: chief executive officer · CFO: chief financial officer
CTA: call to action · EMEA: Europe, the Middle East and Africa · FAQ: frequently asked questions
FYA: for your action · FYR: for your reference · IPO: initial public offering · NDA: non-disclosure agreement
R&D: research and development · SEO: search engine optimization · UX: user experience
RSVP stands for "répondez s'il vous plaît". It is a French phrase that means "please respond".

## 6. Important linking phrases (p. 149–154)

Transitioning from one point to another: furthermore · in addition · however · on the other hand
Emphasizing something: It's important to note that · I'd like to emphasize that · Please keep in mind that · I'd like to highlight that
Rephrasing or clarifying: in other words · to put it differently · to clarify · to rephrase
Providing examples: for instance · for example · as an illustration · such as
Summing up or concluding: in conclusion · in summary · to sum up · overall
Comparing and contrasting: similarly · by contrast · conversely · whereas
Indicating importance: significantly · notably · essentially · above all
Showing cause and effect: due to · because · consequently · therefore · as a result
Expressing agreement or disagreement: I agree that · I disagree with · in my opinion · from my perspective
Providing support or evidence: according to · as stated by · based on · as per
Indicating time or sequence: first · second · next · finally · then · afterward · meanwhile
Adding information: moreover · besides · as well as · along with · not to mention
Clarifying or explaining: that is to say · in other words · namely · specifically
Contrasting: although · despite · while · yet · in spite of · even though
Conditional or hypothetical situations: if · unless · provided that · in case · otherwise
Expressing similarity or comparison: likewise · just as · in the same way · similarly

### Inquiry email
Subject: Inquiry about ___
Dear ___,
I hope this email finds you well. My name is ___, and I am at ___. We recently discovered your ___ and are interested in gathering more information to help us evaluate its suitability for our needs.
To assist us in our assessment, could you kindly provide the following information:
1. A comprehensive overview of the product/service, including its primary features and advantages.
2. Pricing details, including any available discounts, packages or special offers for bulk purchases.
3. Estimated delivery or implementation time frames, as well as any associated shipping or set-up costs.
4. Information regarding warranty, support and maintenance services, if applicable.
We appreciate your prompt attention to this matter and look forward to hearing back from you soon. If you require any additional information from us, please do not hesitate to ask.
Thank you for your time and assistance.
Best regards,
