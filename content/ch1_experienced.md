## 1. Story Four — Interview with an experienced candidate (p. 89–95)
Audio: ex_89

Nashwa turned down the job offer from Modern Technology Corporation and applied for the same job at Tech Genius Corporation. She is currently in an interview with the chief product officer, Mr. Sameh El-Gamal, and the HR manager, Mr. Abdelhady, who will decide whether to accept her or not. They will ask the standard interview questions.

Mr. Abdelhady: Welcome, Ms. Nashwa. I am Abdelhady, the HR manager of Tech Genius Corp. It's nice to meet you.
Nashwa: Nice to meet you too, Mr. Abdelhady.
Mr. Abdelhady: I would like to introduce you to Mr. Sameh El-Gamal. He is the chief product officer of our company, and he will also be conducting this interview with us today.
Nashwa: Nice to meet you, Mr. El-Gamal.
Mr. El-Gamal: Likewise, Ms. Nashwa. I've heard great things about your work, and I'm looking forward to getting to know more about you and your experience.
Mr. Abdelhady: Ms. Nashwa, Mr. El-Gamal and I are part of the team responsible for hiring the director of product development at Tech Genius Corp, and we are excited to learn more about your qualifications and experience. Are you ready to begin the interview?
Nashwa: Yes, I am ready. Thank you for having me.
Mr. El-Gamal: So, can you tell us a little bit about your background and experience in the product development industry?
Nashwa: I have a diverse background in the product development industry, starting as an entry-level analyst and working my way up to my current role as a product manager. In my most recent job, I was responsible for managing the development and release of new software products. I was able to develop my skills in project management, data analysis and market research. I also had the opportunity to lead a team, and I was able to make a significant impact on the company's bottom line by successfully launching several new products. Before that, I worked as a product analyst, where I gained experience in gathering customer feedback, analyzing data and conducting market research. I was able to work on a wide range of projects and develop my skills in project management, problem-solving and communication. I have experience in both software and hardware product development, and I have a track record of successfully launching and managing new products. I am confident that my experience and skills would be an asset to any organization in the product development industry.
Mr. El-Gamal: Impressive. Ms. Nashwa, can you tell us a bit about your personality? How would you describe yourself, and how do you think your personality would benefit the role of director of product development?
Nashwa: I would describe myself as an ambitious, energetic and well-organized individual. I am passionate about product development, and I'm always looking for new and innovative ways to improve our products. I'm a good listener, and I enjoy working with people from different teams and departments to achieve common goals. I am also able to handle pressure and meet deadlines. I believe my personality aligns well with the director of product development role, as I am able to work effectively under pressure and competently communicate and collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver exceptional products.
Mr. El-Gamal: That sounds great, Ms. Nashwa. Can you tell us about a particularly challenging project you've worked on, and how you overcame any obstacles during the development process?
Nashwa: One project that comes to mind was a software product that required integration with several different systems. It was a complex project with many moving parts, and there were multiple teams involved. To overcome these challenges, I implemented a clear project plan and communication strategy, and I also made sure to regularly check in with all team members to ensure everyone was on the same page and that any issues were addressed in a timely manner.
Mr. Abdelhady: Excellent. Generally speaking, Ms. Nashwa, how do you handle challenging or difficult situations in the workplace?
Nashwa: I handle challenging or difficult situations in the workplace by first taking a step back and assessing the situation. I try to understand the root cause of the problem and identify all the potential options for resolving it. I also consult with my team or other stakeholders to gather different perspectives and insights. Once I have a clear understanding of the situation, I develop a plan of action and communicate it clearly to all relevant parties. I also make sure to set realistic timelines and milestones for resolving the issue, and keep everyone informed of the progress. I believe in addressing a problem as soon as it arises and taking a proactive approach to finding a solution.
Mr. Abdelhady: And how do you handle conflicts or differences of opinion with colleagues and superiors?
Nashwa: I believe that conflicts or differences of opinion are a natural part of any work environment, and that they can be an opportunity to find new solutions and improve processes. When I encounter a conflict or difference of opinion with a colleague or superior, my first step is to try to understand the other person's perspective and the reasons behind their position. Then I try to find common ground and identify areas of agreement. I also make sure to remain respectful and professional, and avoid getting defensive or personal. I find that by approaching conflicts this way, I'm able to find mutually beneficial solutions that can help improve our work processes.
Mr. Abdelhady: Can you give us an example of a time when you had a conflict with a colleague or superior, and how you handled it?
Nashwa: Sure, I can give you an example. A few months ago, I was working on a project with a colleague from another department, and we had a disagreement about the direction of the project. They wanted to take a different approach than what I proposed, and I was convinced that my approach was the best one.
Mr. Abdelhady: How did you handle this disagreement?
Nashwa: I first tried to understand their perspective and the reasons behind their position. I listened to their ideas and concerns, and we had a productive conversation. I also made sure to remain respectful and professional, and I avoided getting defensive or personal. In the end, we were able to find common ground and come up with a solution that incorporated the best parts of both of our ideas. It ended up being a win-win situation, and the project was a success.
Mr. El-Gamal: Thanks for sharing that with us. Can you tell us about your approach to staying informed about industry developments and trends?
Nashwa: I stay current with industry developments and trends by staying active in my professional network, attending industry events and conferences, and following relevant news and publications. I also have a regular practice of reading industry-related articles, reports and studies to keep my knowledge up to date. I also have a good relationship with my peers and colleagues; I enjoy discussing the latest developments and trends with them, and I find it helps me understand how other companies are approaching similar challenges and opportunities. In addition, I always take the time to stay informed about my competitors, keeping an eye on their product releases, marketing strategies and customer feedback, to know what they're doing and how they are performing.
Mr. El-Gamal: Ms. Nashwa, how do you see this role as a stepping stone toward your future career aspirations?
Nashwa: My long-term career goal is to become a senior leader in the product development industry. I am eager to take on more responsibility and lead large-scale projects. The role of director of product development is an excellent opportunity for me to achieve this goal, as it would give me the chance to lead the development of new products and improve existing ones. Additionally, this role would give me the opportunity to mentor and lead a team of product managers, which is another aspect of my long-term career goals. I believe the skills and experience I have gained as a product manager, and my passion for product development, make me a strong candidate for this role, and I'm excited about the opportunity to contribute to the growth of the company.
Mr. Abdelhady: Ms. Nashwa, we appreciate you taking the time to speak with us today. We have enjoyed getting to know more about your experience, skills and career goals.
Nashwa: Thank you for having me. I have enjoyed the conversation as well.
Mr. Abdelhady: Before we conclude, is there anything else you would like to share with us, or anything else you think we should know about you?
Nashwa: I believe we've covered everything, but I would like to reiterate my excitement and enthusiasm for the role of director of product development, and how I believe my experience and skills align well with the position.
Mr. Abdelhady: We appreciate your interest and enthusiasm. We will be in touch soon to let you know our decision.
Nashwa: Thank you again for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.
