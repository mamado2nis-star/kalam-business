# Chapter 1 — Phone Interview

## 1. Story 1: The phone call (p. 9–11)
Audio: phone_9

I got a phone call from Sozan Mohsen, who works in Human Resources at AVN's head office, calling me at home in the evening to do a screening interview. She needed to ask me some preliminary questions to decide if I will be invited for a face-to-face interview at AVN.
Sozan: Hello Mr. Khaled, my name is Sozan Mohsen from HR at AVN's head office. I'm wondering if you've got time to answer a few questions about your application?
Khaled: I see, well, I'm actually just about to sit down for dinner, Ms. Sozan.
Khaled: Would you mind if I called you back in half an hour?
Sozan: No, that's fine. I'm in the office for another two hours. The number is 090093232.
Sozan: Thanks for calling me back, Khaled. Now, just to start off with a general question about your background. Can you tell me a little about yourself?
Khaled: I am an enthusiastic engineering college graduate with freelance experience. Completed several client projects, including small consulting and research jobs. Delivered each job under budget and before deadline. I'd describe myself as a dynamic team player, self-disciplined and resourceful, with the ability to adapt well to new situations and thrive on new challenges.
Sozan: Uh huh, interesting, so what are you looking for in your first job?
Khaled: I've been honing my skills for a few years now, and first and foremost I'm looking for a position where I can exercise those skills. I'm definitely looking for a position where I can grow. Professional development is something that's really important to me, since I hope to take on managerial responsibilities in the future.
Sozan: Uh huh. Now, can you tell me a little about your salary expectations for this position?
Khaled: Well, Ms. Sozan, I'm looking for somewhere between $3,000 and $5,000 for this role. But for me, benefits matter as well. Your training programs, the commuter benefits and other perks could definitely allow me to be a bit flexible with salary.
Sozan: Okay, thank you so much for your time, Khaled. Have you got any last-minute questions?
Khaled: Yes. What are the next steps in the interview process?
Sozan: Well, if you are short-listed, we will contact you for the panel interview within the next few days. Thanks for talking to me today, Khaled. Thanks for your time.
Khaled: Good-bye, Sozan.

## 4. Why are you looking for a new job? (p. 21–24)

### The questions

### Reasons for looking for a new job
I am looking for a company that values me, my skills and my experience more.
I'm searching for better compensation.
I want a more senior role.
I am ready for a new challenge.
My role changed over time and I was no longer doing what I was interested in.
I want to develop and explore different interests or skills.
I'd like different job duties.
I want to work in a different field or industry.
I didn't feel I had any more opportunity to grow or further my career, so I decided to look for a change.
My boss and I have different work styles, values or priorities.
I thrive more in a different kind of work environment / I want to find a healthier, non-toxic environment.
My company is pursuing a different strategy, restructuring, or being acquired by or merged with another organization.
I am looking for an employer that provides more training, development or feedback.
I reassessed my career goals and decided my current role no longer fits them.
I'm searching for a hybrid, remote or in-office setup, or hours my current job doesn't allow.
I love my role and co-workers, but I've come to a point where there are no longer growth opportunities for me there.
I am relocating for non-work reasons (family, etc.).
### Sample answers
During my three years at ___, I had the opportunity to develop a strong skill set in ___. It was a great learning experience, and I enjoyed contributing to the team. Now I'm ready to join a company that values my skills and allows me to use them more fully.
I realized the leadership of my team was going in a different direction, and I'm interested in working in a more collaborative environment. It was a hard decision to make, because I love the mission of the company, but I ultimately think this is the right choice.
I'm ready for the next challenge in my career. I loved the people I worked with and the projects I worked on, but at some point I realized I wasn't being challenged the way I used to be. Rather than let myself get too comfortable, I decided to pursue a position where I can continue to grow.
I realized that my true passion is working as ___. I've always loved ___, but decided to take my career in a different direction. Now I'm ready to pursue ___, and I'm particularly excited by the possibility of working with ___.
In my last role, the company suffered some financial hardships and had to eliminate my job, along with many others. I'm grateful for my time in that role, as it gave me the opportunity to increase my knowledge while forging lifelong bonds with my colleagues.
I've learned a lot in my current role, but now I'm looking to take on more responsibility, as I want to continue developing my skills and abilities — particularly my management abilities. I'm excited that this role provides the opportunity to build out a team rather than only being the sole contributor.

### Avoiding giving a salary figure
At this point in my job search, I'm focusing on finding the position that's the best fit for my skills and career. Once I've done that, I'm willing to consider an offer that you feel is fair for the role.
Perhaps you could give me an idea of the pay range for this post?
I'm interested in finding a job that is a good fit for me. Actually, I don't have a specific number in mind, so I'd feel more comfortable if you could give me an idea of the salary range.
I want this move to be a big step forward for me in terms of both responsibility and compensation. I don't have a specific figure, so could you give me an idea of the current salary for this position?
I'm currently earning a base salary of ___. I don't have a specific number in mind that I'm targeting for this next position, though, and I'm willing to consider an offer that you feel is fair.

My first priority is to gain knowledge and improve my skills. I don't have any expectations about salary, but I want to become financially independent, so I will accept as per company standards.

I'm more interested in finding a position that's a good fit for my skills and interests. I'm confident that you're offering a salary that's competitive in the current market.
