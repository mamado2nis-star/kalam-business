# Chapter 1 — Interview · Internal interview

## 1. Story Three — Internal interview (p. 68–75)
Audio: in_68

Nashwa is a five-year veteran of the product development industry. She currently works as a product manager for Modern Technology Corporation (MTC), the leading provider of software solutions. Nashwa is looking for a new challenge and is excited about the chance to take on more responsibility as the director of product development at the same company. She will be interviewed by the HR manager, Mr. Hossam, who will ask questions about her experience, skills and career goals. Let's begin the interview.

Mr. Hossam: Ms. Nashwa, thanks for taking the time to be with me today.
Nashwa: Thanks for having me, Mr. Hossam.
Mr. Hossam: Having a good day so far?
Nashwa: Yes, sure. What about you?
Mr. Hossam: I'm doing great. So, can you tell me a little bit about your current role at the company?
Nashwa: Sure. I currently work as a product manager. I'm in charge of managing the creation and release of new products, as well as the improvement and upkeep of the ones we already have.
Mr. Hossam: That sounds interesting. Can you tell me more about what a typical day looks like for you?
Nashwa: My days are usually quite varied. I spend a lot of time meeting with cross-functional teams to discuss new product ideas and gather feedback. I also work on creating product roadmaps and plans, and tracking the progress of projects to ensure we stay on schedule.
Mr. Hossam: That sounds like a lot of responsibility. What skills do you think are important for someone in your role to have?
Nashwa: I think it's important for a product manager to have strong communication skills, as well as the ability to think strategically and solve problems. It's also helpful to have a good understanding of the technical aspects of product development, as well as the business side of things.
Mr. Hossam: Thank you for sharing. Do you have any experience with data analysis or market research?
Nashwa: Yes, I have some experience with both. In my previous role, I worked on a project where I analyzed customer data to identify trends and areas for improvement. I've also conducted market research to gather insights on competitor products and industry trends.
Mr. Hossam: Thank you for sharing your experience and skills with me. Can you tell me about the strengths you bring to your role as a product manager?
Nashwa: One of my strengths is my ability to think creatively and come up with new ideas. I enjoy coming up with innovative solutions to problems, and I've found this to be a valuable skill in my role as a product manager.
Mr. Hossam: It's great to hear. Would you also share a weakness you feel you have, and how you're working to improve it?
Nashwa: One weakness I feel I have is my tendency to get overwhelmed by large, complex projects. In the past, I've struggled with managing my time effectively and staying organized. To improve this, I have been using project-management tools and techniques to better plan and track my work. I've also been trying to delegate tasks more effectively, to make sure I don't take on too much at once.
Mr. Hossam: Thank you for being honest and self-aware. It's important to recognize our weaknesses and work on improving them. Do you have any other strengths or areas of expertise you'd like to share?
Nashwa: One other strength I have is my ability to build and maintain relationships with cross-functional teams. I'm a good communicator and enjoy working with people from different departments to achieve common goals. I've found this is crucial in my role as a product manager, as I rely on many different teams to bring products to market.
Mr. Hossam: That's amazing. Would you tell me about a notable achievement or accomplishment that you're proud of from your time at the company?
Nashwa: One achievement I'm particularly proud of is the successful launch of a new product whose development I led. The project had a lot of complexity, and there were many challenges we had to overcome during development. However, we were able to stay on schedule and deliver a high-quality product that met the needs of our customers. The product has done well on the market, and it has made a big difference in how much money the company makes.
Mr. Hossam: That's impressive. Can you tell me more about your role in the project and how you contributed to its success?
Nashwa: As the product manager, it was my job to oversee the whole development process and make sure all the cross-functional teams were on the same page and working toward the same goals. I also worked closely with the engineering team to troubleshoot any issues that arose, and with the marketing team to develop the go-to-market strategy. Overall, I think the success of the project was due in large part to the fact that I am good at managing projects and can communicate well with the team.
Mr. Hossam: That's very impressive. It sounds like you made a significant contribution to the company's success. Thank you for sharing that with me. Do you have any other notable achievements you'd like to share?
Nashwa: Thank you. Yes — another achievement I'm proud of is the development of the customer feedback program that I led. We were able to gather valuable insights from our customers that helped us improve the quality of our products to better meet their needs. The program has been well received by both customers and internal teams, and it has contributed to the overall satisfaction of our customers.
Mr. Hossam: I appreciate you telling me about your accomplishments. Could you explain your interest in this new position with the company?
Nashwa: I am excited about the prospect of assuming increased responsibilities and stretching myself in a new role. My tenure as a product manager has been both rewarding and fulfilling. However, I have come to recognize that I've reached a plateau in terms of my experience and personal growth. I am eager to embrace new challenges, and I am firmly convinced that this new role would give me the opportunity to do so, enabling me to continue to learn and advance in my profession.
Mr. Hossam: That's great to hear. Can you tell me more about your long-term career goals and how this new role fits into them?
Nashwa: My long-term career goal is to become a leader in the field of product development and management. I believe this new role would give me the opportunity to gain valuable experience and expertise in the field, and to develop the skills and knowledge I need to achieve my career goals. I'm excited about the opportunity to contribute to the success of the company and to make a positive impact in my field.
Mr. Hossam: That's very ambitious, and I appreciate your dedication to your career goals. Can you tell me more about any new ideas you have for the company or the team you'll be joining?
Nashwa: I always try to be proactive and think creatively about how we can improve our products and processes. I believe there are always opportunities to innovate and find new ways of doing things. One idea I've been thinking about is implementing a customer feedback program for our products. I believe that by regularly gathering and analyzing customer feedback, we can identify areas for improvement and better meet the needs of our customers.
Mr. Hossam: Thank you, Ms. Nashwa, for sharing your new ideas and suggestions with me. It's clear you have a proactive approach and are willing to bring fresh perspectives to the table. I think you'd be a great fit for the new role, and I'm confident you'll make a positive contribution to the team.
Nashwa: Thank you, Mr. Hossam, for the opportunity to interview for the new role. I'm excited about the opportunity to take on more responsibility and challenge myself, and I'm eager to contribute my skills and experience to the team.
Mr. Hossam: Great. Well, I think we've covered everything. Thank you again for taking the time to be with me today. I'll be in touch soon with the next steps.
Nashwa: Thank you, Mr. Hossam, for the opportunity to interview. I look forward to hearing from you and learning about the next steps in the process.
Mr. Hossam: You're welcome. Have a great day.
Nashwa: You too. Thanks again.
