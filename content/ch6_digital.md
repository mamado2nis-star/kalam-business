# Chapter 6 — Digital Business Communications

## 1. Virtual meetings: experiencing technical difficulties (p. 266–271)
Audio: dg_266

### Experiencing technical difficulties
The following are common situations that people may encounter while using virtual meeting platforms.
Situation: Poor internet connection
I'm experiencing some connectivity issues. Please bear with me while I try to resolve them.
Situation: Frozen video
It seems my video has frozen. I'll try turning off my camera and turning it back on.
Situation: Audio not working
I can't hear anyone. Can you please confirm if you can hear me? I'll attempt to fix my audio settings.
Situation: Microphone not working
It seems my microphone isn't working. Let me quickly troubleshoot and get back to you.
Situation: Difficulty sharing screen
I'm having trouble sharing my screen. Give me a moment to sort this out, or could someone else share the presentation in the meantime?
Situation: Unexpected disconnection from the meeting
Apologies for the sudden disconnection. I experienced a technical issue and am now back online.
Situation: Echo or feedback issues
It seems there's an echo or feedback coming from someone's audio. Can everyone please check if their microphones are muted when not speaking?
Situation: Slow or lagging video
Apologies if my video is lagging or slow. My internet connection seems to be a bit unstable at the moment. I'll try to switch to a better connection.
Situation: Need assistance with platform features
I'm not familiar with this platform's features. Can someone please assist me?
Situation: Difficulty accessing meeting files or documents
I'm having trouble accessing the shared files. Could someone please resend the link or email the documents to me?
Situation: Unable to play a video or audio clip during a presentation
I'm having difficulty playing the video/audio. Let me try again, or I'll share the link for everyone to view separately.
Situation: Device running out of battery
My device's battery is running low. Allow me a moment to plug it in.
Situation: Need to switch devices or platforms
I'm experiencing issues with my current device/platform. Please give me a few minutes to switch to another device/platform.
Situation: Need to restart the device or application
I'm facing some technical issues. I'll need to quickly restart my device/application and rejoin the meeting.
### Helping someone with turning on their microphone
It seems your microphone is off. You can enable it by clicking on the microphone icon at the bottom of the screen. If it has a red line across it, that means it's muted.
### Assisting someone with turning on their video
Your video appears to be off. To turn it on, click on the video camera icon at the bottom of the screen. If the icon has a red line across it, that means your video is currently disabled.
### Reminding someone to mute their microphone
Kindly mute your microphone when you're not speaking to minimize background noise. You can do so by clicking the microphone icon at the bottom of your screen.
### Asking someone to unmute their microphone
It seems your microphone is muted. To unmute it, please click on the microphone icon at the bottom of your screen, and we'll be able to hear you.
Situation: An attendee's audio or video is cutting out
It appears you might be experiencing some internet issues, as your audio or video is breaking up. If possible, you may want to check your connection or try reconnecting to the meeting.
Could you try turning off your video to improve the connection quality?

## 2. Virtual meetings: using chat functions and handling disruptions (p. 272–274)
Audio: dg_272

### Using chat functions
Situation: Sharing a link or document in the chat
I'm posting a link to the relevant documents in the chat for everyone to access.
Situation: Encouraging participants to use the chat for questions
Please feel free to use the chat function to ask any questions during the presentation. We'll address them at the end.
Situation: Asking someone to re-share information in the chat
Could you please repost the link or information in the chat? It seems to have gotten lost in the earlier messages.
Situation: Addressing an off-topic conversation in the chat
Let's try to keep the chat focused on the current topic. If you have unrelated questions or comments, please save them for later or send them in a private message.
### Handling disruptions or interactions in online meetings
Situation: A participant unintentionally interrupting the speaker
It looks like ___ has a question or comment, but let's allow the current speaker to finish their point first. We'll address any questions or comments afterward.
Situation: An attendee joining the meeting late
Welcome, ___! We're glad you could join us. We're currently discussing ___. Please mute your microphone and feel free to catch up through the chat or shared materials.
Situation: Off-topic conversation derailing the meeting
While the current discussion is interesting, let's refocus on the main topic at hand. We can address any off-topic questions or comments at a later time.
Situation: Multiple participants speaking at the same time
It seems multiple people are trying to speak at once. Let's use the "raise hand" feature or type questions in the chat to avoid talking over each other.
Situation: Excessive background noise or distractions affecting the meeting
There's some background noise coming through. Can everyone please ensure they're in a quiet space and that their microphones are muted when not speaking?
Situation: A participant dominating the conversation or interrupting others
We appreciate your enthusiasm. Let's make sure everyone has a chance to contribute. We can return to your point later if there's time.

## 3. Virtual meetings: different situations (p. 275–277)
Audio: dg_275

### Different situations
Situation: Joining a meeting late
I'm just joining the call. Could you please give me a quick recap?
Situation: Leaving the meeting
I have another meeting right after this, so I may need to leave a few minutes early.
Situation: Managing time
In the interest of time, let's limit our responses to one minute each.
Situation: Encouraging camera usage
If possible, please turn on your cameras to help create a more engaging and connected experience.
Situation: Asking for permission to share your screen
Could you please grant me permission to share my screen? I'd like to present some visuals to support our discussion.
Situation: Asking someone to repeat their point due to audio cutting out
Your audio cut out for a moment. Could you please repeat your last point?
Situation: Prompting quiet or less engaged participants to contribute to the discussion
We haven't heard from ___ yet. Do you have any thoughts or opinions you'd like to share on this topic?
Situation: Informing participants that the meeting will be recorded
Please note that we'll be recording today's meeting for reference purposes and to share with those who couldn't attend. By continuing in this meeting, you consent to being recorded.
Situation: Announcing the use of breakout rooms in a meeting
We'll be using breakout rooms today to facilitate smaller group discussions on the topic. Each group will have fifteen minutes to brainstorm and share ideas.
Situation: Assigning participants to breakout rooms
I will now assign everyone to their respective breakout rooms. Please check your screen for an invitation to join your designated room.

Situation: Congratulating the client on a milestone or achievement

Situation: Inviting the client to an event or webinar

Situation: Announcing the completion of a project or task

Situation: Requesting additional information from the client

Situation: Asking for a referral

Situation: Requesting payment or following up on an overdue invoice
Just a friendly reminder that invoice #___ is due on ___. If you've already made a payment, please disregard this message. Otherwise, we kindly ask that you submit your payment as soon as possible.
Situation: Introducing a new product or service
We're excited to announce the launch of our new ___, which we believe could greatly benefit your business. If you're interested in learning more, please let us know, and we'll provide additional information.
