# Chapter 3 — Business Meetings

## 1. Story 1: The rescue meeting (p. 179–186)
Audio: mt_179

The following business meeting focuses on addressing the financial challenges and finding solutions for the future of the company.
The attendees, consisting of the marketing director, operations director, financial director, human resources director and sales director, engage in a formal discussion led by the financial director.
Through debate, negotiation, suggestions and the occasional interruption, the attendees engage in a dynamic conversation, utilizing common expressions and phrases commonly used in a business English setting.
Financial Director: Good morning, everyone. It is a pleasure to have you all here today for this critical meeting.
Financial Director: The purpose of this meeting is to address the financial challenges that our company has been facing, and to collaboratively formulate a comprehensive solution that will secure the long-term stability of the organization.
Financial Director: Given the urgency of the situation, I would like to express my gratitude for your prompt attendance and readiness to engage in a productive discussion.
All: Good morning.
Financial Director: I would like to introduce everyone in the room. On my left is the marketing director, Ms. Nadia Youssef.
Financial Director: To her left is the operations director, Mr. Mohamed Ali.
Financial Director: And on my right is the human resources director, Mr. Ibrahim Yehia.
Financial Director: To his right is the sales director, Ms. Rofaida Abdelaziz.
All: Hello, everyone.
Financial Director: We're all here today because we're facing a very serious issue. Our company has been struggling financially for several months, and we need to find a solution quickly.
Financial Director: I've asked each of you to come today to share your thoughts and opinions on how we can rescue the company.
Ms. Nadia: Yes, the situation is not good. We can't afford to wait any longer. We need to act fast.
Financial Director: I couldn't agree more, Ms. Nadia. Our goal today is to find a solution that will take us out of this financial crisis and put us back on the path to success.
Mr. Ibrahim: I have some ideas that I'd like to share with the group.
Financial Director: Absolutely, Mr. Ibrahim. Please go ahead.
Mr. Ibrahim: I think we should focus on cutting costs in all departments, starting with reducing the workforce and trimming our expenses.
Ms. Rofaida: I understand the importance of cost-cutting. However, I would like to suggest that we also place equal emphasis on finding new business opportunities and expanding our customer base as a means of increasing revenue.
Mr. Mohamed: I have to agree with Ms. Rofaida. We can't just focus on cutting costs. We need to also focus on growth.
Ms. Nadia: I think we need to look at both options and find a balance between reducing costs and increasing revenue.
Financial Director: That's a great suggestion, Nadia. Can anyone else provide their thoughts on this?
Mr. Ibrahim: I think it would be useful to get input from the rest of the departments and see how they feel about cutting costs and increasing revenue.
Financial Director: I think that's a good idea. Mohamed, would you be able to organize a survey and gather the results for us?
Mr. Mohamed: Of course. I'll get right on that.
Ms. Rofaida: I have a question for clarification. How will we define success with this plan?
Financial Director: That's a good question, Ms. Rofaida. Our success will be measured by a significant improvement in our financial situation and a return to profitability.
Ms. Nadia: I'd like to suggest that we set a specific target for the end of the fiscal year.
Mr. Ibrahim: I agree with Nadia. We need a clear target to measure our progress and success.
Financial Director: All right. Let's set a target of a 10% increase in revenue by the end of the fiscal year. Does everyone agree with this target?
Ms. Rofaida: I understand the importance of setting a target, but I'm not sure that a 10% increase in revenue is realistic in our current situation.
Ms. Nadia: But we need to aim high in order to push ourselves to achieve more.
Mr. Mohamed: I agree with Ms. Rofaida. I think we need to be cautious and consider the risks involved in setting such an ambitious goal.
Financial Director: I understand your concerns, but I believe that with proper planning and execution, we can achieve this target.
Financial Director: Let's take a moment to consider different viewpoints and try to find a solution that everyone can agree on. Does anyone have an alternative proposal?
The discussion continued, with various points of view and suggestions being exchanged, until a compromise was reached.
The group agreed to aim for a 5% increase in revenue, with a plan in place to reassess and adjust the target if necessary.
Financial Director: I have a question for everyone. What do you think about offering a new product line to increase revenue?
Ms. Nadia: I think it's a good idea. We should consider the market demand and feasibility of this new product line.
Ms. Rofaida: I agree. We should also consider the costs involved in developing and launching a new product line.
Mr. Mohamed: I think we should get input from the sales and marketing departments and see what they think about the potential success of this new product line.
Financial Director: I think that's a good suggestion, Mr. Mohamed. All right, let's put that on the action item list for Ms. Nadia and Ms. Rofaida to follow up with their departments and come up with a plan.
Mr. Ibrahim: I have another suggestion. What about entering into partnerships with other companies to increase our reach and market share?
Ms. Nadia: That could be a good option, but we need to consider the impact it may have on our brand image.
Ms. Rofaida: I agree with Ibrahim. We need to carefully vet any potential partners and make sure they align with our values and mission.
Financial Director: Let's put that on the action item list for Ibrahim and Nadia to explore and bring back to the group for further discussion.
At this point, the meeting is running long, and some attendees are starting to lose focus.
Financial Director: Ladies and gentlemen, if I could have your attention for a moment. We've been discussing some great ideas, but I think it's time to regroup and summarize where we're at.
Mr. Mohamed: I agree. Let's take a quick break and come back in ten minutes.
Financial Director: That's a good idea. Let's reconvene in ten minutes to continue our discussion.
After the break, the meeting continues with renewed energy and focus.
Financial Director: Welcome back, everyone. Let's continue where we left off.
Ms. Rofaida: I'd like to propose an idea. What about offering promotions and discounts to our current customers to increase loyalty and repeat business?
Ms. Nadia: I think that's a good idea. We should also consider offering incentives to our sales team to drive more business.
Financial Director: I concur. Let's include this on the action item list for Rofaida to investigate and present to the team for additional examination.
At the end of the meeting, the attendees vote on the final rescue plan for the company: a combination of reducing costs, increasing revenue and exploring new business opportunities.
Financial Director: All right, let's move on to the final rescue plan for the company. All in favour, raise your hands.
All the attendees raise their hands.
Financial Director: The motion is carried. Thank you, everyone, for your contributions and participation in this meeting. Let's get to work on implementing this plan and securing the future of this company.

## 9. Short dialogue 3: the marketing strategy meeting (p. 209–211)
Audio: mt_209

Manager: Good afternoon, everyone. Thank you for joining us today.
Manager: Our agenda today is to discuss the marketing strategy for our new product launch.
Manager: Let's start by asking for opinions and feedback from everyone. Does anyone have any thoughts on the current plan we've outlined?
Employee 1: I have a few suggestions regarding the target audience for our marketing campaign. I think we should focus more on younger consumers.
Manager: Great, thank you for that feedback. Can we clarify what you mean by younger consumers?
Employee 1: I mean those who are between the ages of 18 and 35.
Manager: I see. Any other questions or clarifications before we move on to the next topic?
Employee 2: Yes, I have a question about the budget for the marketing campaign. Can you clarify how much we have allocated for this?
Manager: Of course. The budget for the marketing campaign is $500,000.
Employee 3: I'm sorry to interrupt, but I'd like to bring up a related issue. What about the revenue projections for the next quarter? Can we clarify the numbers?
Manager: Of course, we'll be happy to clarify. Let's continue with our discussion, and we will address that after we finish this section.
Manager: Moving on, does anyone have any suggestions for how we can allocate the budget for the marketing campaign?
Employee 1: I suggest that we consider allocating more towards digital advertising. With the current trend of people spending more time online, I think it would be a more effective way to reach our target audience.
Manager: That's a good suggestion. Does anyone else have any recommendations?
Employee 2: That's a good idea. However, I think we should also allocate some budget towards traditional advertising methods, such as print and television ads.
Manager: Okay, let's put it to a vote. All in favor of allocating more towards digital advertising, raise your hand.
Manager: And all in favor of allocating some budget towards traditional advertising methods, raise your hand.
Manager: The vote is in favor of allocating more towards digital advertising.
Manager: Finally, let's wrap up and close this meeting. Thank you, everyone, for your time and input.
Manager: The next steps are to implement the marketing strategy and to meet again in two weeks to review our progress.
Manager: Any final comments or questions?
Employee 1: No, thank you for the meeting. Everything is clear. Everything is OK.
Manager: All right, that concludes today's meeting. Have a good day, everyone.
